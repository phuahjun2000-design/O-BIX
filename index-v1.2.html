Technical specifications

Trako v1.3 — current shipping hardware revision.

Appearance changes

  • · 3cm × 3cm square board — 54% smaller footprint than the v1.2 5cm disc
  • · Side-mounted buttons A / B
  • · Dedicated hardware power switch

Hardware changes

  • · MPU6050 replaced by BMI270 (consumer-grade IMU)
  • · MCP73831 battery charging IC
  • · LTC2954-2 power switch controller IC
  • · UART header — future peripherals connect with two wires
  • · Status LED — reports hardware health and battery level

Status LED — planned states

Solid green
System nominal, battery healthy
Slow amber blink
Battery low — charge before flight
Fast red blink
Hardware fault — do not launch

Why we built this

Traditional motion-tracking instruments are often expensive, bulky, and locked to proprietary software. O∑BIX started as a small group of friends who wanted an affordable way to capture real flight and impact data — without lab-grade equipment. Trako is what came out of it: a compact sensor that streams live acceleration, orientation, and altitude data straight to a browser, built to be flown, dropped, and flown again.
