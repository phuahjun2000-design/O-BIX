Trako 2.0

Round, 5 cm. Power it from USB-C, or from a 9V battery for experiments on the move.

TRAKO 3.0 · NEWEST

Trako 3.0

The same job on a board 54% smaller, with side buttons, a status LED and an expansion port.

  • · 3cm × 3cm square board
  • · BMI270 motion sensor on an ESP32-S3
  • · Buttons A, B and a power switch on the side
  • · MCP73831 charging, LTC2954-2 power control
  • · LED shows hardware health and battery level
  • · UART port: add peripherals with two data wires

Technical specifications

What changed from Trako 2.0 to Trako 3.0, and the software behind both.

Appearance changes

  • · Shrunk to a 3cm × 3cm square — 54% less area than the 5 cm disc
  • · Side user buttons A and B
  • · Dedicated power switch

Hardware changes

  • · MPU6050 replaced by BMI270 (consumer-grade chip)
  • · MCP73831 as the charging IC
  • · LTC2954-2 as the power-switch control IC
  • · UART port — extend with peripherals using just two data wires
  • · Status LED showing hardware health and battery level
